About Me

I'm Samarth Shukla, a Software and AI Engineer and a Master's student in Software Engineering (Scalable Systems) at Carnegie Mellon University's School of Computer Science.

My work lives where research becomes product: WebGPU inference in the browser, agentic CLI workflows, evaluation harnesses on FastAPI and MongoDB, and export tooling that packages fine-tuned models for CUDA, Apple silicon, and edge backends.

That work has been recognized by the Meta Llama Impact Grant and NVIDIA Inception, published at IEEE and TechRxiv, and proven across nine national and one international hackathon win. I care about systems that are reliable, private by default, and ready for production.

Based in Pittsburgh. Built in Indore. Shipping software that holds up under load.
